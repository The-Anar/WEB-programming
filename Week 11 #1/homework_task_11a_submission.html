Implementation

The StudentProfileCard component receives five props:

  • name — student name
  • studentId — student identification number
  • major — academic major
  • year — study year
  • gpa — grade point average

The App component stores student information in an array and uses .map() to render three reusable StudentProfileCard components. Each card has a unique key.

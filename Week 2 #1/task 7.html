```html Mongolian Buuz
Mongolian Buuz (Бууз)

What You Need

  • 500 g ground beef or mutton
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 cup water, approximately

Steps

  1. Mix the flour and water to make a firm dough. Knead it well and let it rest.
  2. Mix the ground meat, chopped onions, garlic, salt, and black pepper in a bowl.
  3. Roll the dough into a long shape and cut it into small pieces.
  4. Roll each piece into a thin, round wrapper.
  5. Put a spoonful of meat filling in the center of each wrapper.
  6. Fold the edges together and pinch them closed to form a buuz.
  7. Warning: Be careful when using a knife and handling hot steam.
  8. Place the buuz in a steamer, leaving space between them.
  9. Steam for about 20–25 minutes, until the meat is fully cooked.
  10. Serve the buuz hot and enjoy this traditional Mongolian dish.
```

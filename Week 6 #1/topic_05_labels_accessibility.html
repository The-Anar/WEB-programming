Why Are Labels Important?

Labels explain what information a form field needs. They also help screen-reader users identify inputs and let users click the label to focus its field.

Method 1: Match label's for and input's id

Method 2: Put the input inside the label

Incorrect: Placeholder without a label

Placeholder text disappears when you type and is not a replacement for a proper label.

Accessible Registration Example

Key Points

  • Connect label for="field-id" to input id="field-id".
  • The label's for and input's id must match exactly.
  • Clicking a connected label focuses its input.
  • Screen readers use labels to identify form controls.
  • Placeholders are hints, not replacements for labels.

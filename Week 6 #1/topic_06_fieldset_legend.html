What Are Fieldset and Legend?

The <fieldset> element groups related form controls together.

The <legend> element provides a title for that group.

Remember: Use fieldsets to organize forms into clear sections, especially when a form has several types of information.

Example: Student Registration Form

Personal Information
Account Information

Key Points

  • <fieldset> groups related form controls.
  • <legend> names the fieldset group.
  • Use separate fieldsets for different sections.
  • Use labels connected to their inputs for accessibility.
  • Fieldsets can improve form structure and readability.

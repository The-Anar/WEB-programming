WEEK 08 · WEB ACCESSIBILITY

Color Contrast

Good contrast helps people read text and distinguish interface elements.

What Is Contrast?

Contrast is the difference in brightness between foreground content and its background.

WCAG AA Contrast Ratios

  • Normal text: at least 4.5:1.
  • Large text: at least 3:1.

Example

Dark text on a white background is generally easy to read.

Don't Rely on Color Alone

For example, don't indicate an error using only a red border. Include a clear error message as well.

How to Check

Use a color contrast checker to compare the text and background colors.

Learning Overview

Web accessibility focuses on making websites usable by people with different abilities and ways of interacting with technology.

  • Build meaningful page structures with semantic HTML.
  • Choose native interactive elements over unnecessary custom controls.
  • Provide appropriate alternative text for images.
  • Use ARIA attributes when additional accessibility information is needed.
  • Support keyboard navigation and visible focus.
  • Maintain sufficient color contrast for readable content.

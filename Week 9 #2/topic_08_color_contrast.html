WEEK 9.2 · TOPIC 08

Color Contrast

Color contrast is the difference in brightness between text and its background. Good contrast makes information easier to read, especially for people with visual impairments.

WCAG REQUIREMENTS

Understanding Contrast Ratios

WCAG uses contrast ratios to measure how clearly text can be distinguished from its background. A ratio ranges from 1:1 to 21:1.

WCAG levelNormal textLarge text
AA4.5:13:1
AAA7:14.5:1

Large text generally means at least 18pt regular text or 14pt bold text (approximately 24px and 18.67px).

FAILING EXAMPLES

Low Contrast Combinations

Example 01

Light pink text on white

Colors: #ff9999 on #ffffff

Contrast: Approximately 2.0:1

Fails WCAG AA for normal text and large text.

Example 02

Orange text on white

Colors: #ff9900 on #ffffff

Contrast: Approximately 2.1:1

Fails WCAG AA for normal text and large text.

PASSING EXAMPLES

Better Contrast Combinations

Example 01 — AA

Dark gray text on white

Colors: #767676 on #ffffff

Contrast: Approximately 4.5:1

Meets the normal text AA requirement.

Example 02 — AAA

Darker gray text on white

Colors: #595959 on #ffffff

Contrast: Approximately 7:1

Meets the normal text AAA requirement.

Example 03 — AA

White text on dark green

Colors: #ffffff on #1a7f5a

Contrast: Approximately 5:1

Meets the normal text AA requirement.

PRACTICAL RULES

How to Improve Color Contrast

  • Use dark text on light backgrounds or light text on dark backgrounds.
  • Avoid light gray text on white backgrounds.
  • Check text contrast before publishing a website.
  • Do not use color alone to communicate important information.
  • Make sure links and interactive controls are easy to identify.
  • Test both light and dark themes.

Some content, such as decorative graphics and inactive interface components, has different contrast requirements or may be exempt under WCAG.

KEY POINTS

What We Learned

  • Contrast helps people read and understand text.
  • WCAG AA requires 4.5:1 for normal text.
  • WCAG AA requires 3:1 for large text.
  • WCAG AAA requires 7:1 for normal text.
  • Light text on a light background is difficult to read.
  • Contrast tools help developers find accessibility problems.
  • Always test colors in both light and dark themes.

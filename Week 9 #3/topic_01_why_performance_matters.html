Why Performance Matters

Fast sites win. Website speed affects user experience, engagement, and business results. Even a small delay can make a difference.

THE BIG PICTURE

Why Website Performance Matters

Speed is part of the user experience

Visitors expect websites to respond quickly. When a page takes too long to load, users may leave before they can see or interact with its content.

Performance is not only about making a website feel faster. It can also affect conversions and revenue.

THE NUMBERS

Performance Statistics

The lecture highlights several important numbers showing why loading speed matters.

53%

Mobile users abandon slow sites

53% of mobile users leave a website that takes more than 3 seconds to load.

7%

Conversion drop

A 1-second delay is associated with a 7% drop in conversions (Amazon).

1%

Revenue drop

A 100-millisecond delay is associated with a 1% revenue drop (Google).

3 seconds

The abandonment threshold

The slide identifies 3 seconds as the point where 53% of mobile users leave.

The goal: 0.1 seconds. The lecture describes this response time as feeling "instant" to users.

MEASURING PERFORMANCE

Google Lighthouse — 5 Audit Categories

Lighthouse evaluates a website using five categories. Each category provides a different view of website quality.

Score: 0–100

Performance

Measures loading and page performance. The slide mentions LCP, FID, CLS, and other Core Web Vitals-related metrics.

Score: 0–100

Accessibility

Checks accessibility features such as color contrast, labels, and ARIA attributes.

Score: 0–100

Best Practices

Checks items such as HTTPS, console errors, and image aspect ratios.

Score: 0–100

SEO

Checks search engine optimization items such as meta tags, robots.txt, and mobile-friendly design.

Pass / Fail

PWA

Checks Progressive Web App features such as a service worker and manifest. The slide describes this category as optional.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

What We Learned

  • Website speed affects user experience.
  • 53% of mobile users may leave when loading takes more than 3 seconds.
  • Even small delays can affect conversions and revenue.
  • Google Lighthouse has five audit categories.
  • A Performance score of 90–100 is in the Good category.

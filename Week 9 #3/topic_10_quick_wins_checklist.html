03 — Final Review

Website performance optimization is not just one technique. It combines several improvements that work together.

  • Optimize images and reduce unnecessary downloads.
  • Load JavaScript without unnecessarily blocking HTML parsing.
  • Prevent layout shifts by defining image dimensions.
  • Reduce transferred data through compression and minification.
  • Use caching and CDNs to deliver resources efficiently.
  • Measure and review performance using browser DevTools.

Small improvements across several areas can make a noticeable difference to the user experience.

WCAG Levels

WCAG provides guidance for making web content more accessible.

Learning Notes

  • Level A: addresses basic accessibility requirements.
  • Level AA: the common target for accessibility compliance.
  • Level AAA: the highest and most demanding level.
  • Normal text generally needs a 4.5:1 contrast ratio for WCAG AA.
  • Large text generally needs a 3:1 contrast ratio for WCAG AA.

Review the examples, then explain how the concepts improve accessibility for users.
